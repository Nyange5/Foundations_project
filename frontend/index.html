Go anywhere in Rwanda

Bus tickets, private cars and shared seats between districts. One search, with the price shown before you book.

Ways to travel

Bus

Compare departures across bus companies and buy your ticket without going to the terminal first.

Private car

A car for you and your group to another district. You see the price before you confirm.

Shared seat

Take a seat in a private car already going your way and split the cost of the trip.

Book a trip in three steps

  1. Search your route

    Choose where you are leaving from, where you are going and when.

  2. Compare and choose

    See buses, private cars and shared seats side by side, with prices.

  3. Pay and travel

    Pay with mobile money and show your ticket on the day.

Drive or run buses with ETIKE

Drivers

Offer the empty seats on your long trips and reach passengers who are already looking for a ride.

Bus companies

List your routes and departure times where passengers compare their options.
